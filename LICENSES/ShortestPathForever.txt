DropOff's walking router, its walking maps and the heights it gives a hand-in
are Shortest Path Forever's. Taken from release v1.43.2, commit
834cc7c3be85f862bf0ef5db7410c57c439f36d1, on 2026-11-18:

  Routing/PathGrid.lua, Path.lua, PathJobs.lua   Router/                         the router
  Data/Heights.lua                               Router/Heights.lua              spawns over or under another floor
  Nav/Nav0*.lua                                  DropOff_Paths_EasternKingdoms/  walking maps
  Nav/Nav1*.lua                                  DropOff_Paths_Kalimdor/
  Nav/Nav2991*.lua                               DropOff_Paths_Zephras/

What was changed in those files: a four-line notice on top of each, and the
global the maps are kept in, ShortestPathForeverPathData, is named
DropOffPathData, so that both addons can be installed side by side. Nothing
else. tools/vendor-router.sh rebuilds them from a checkout and compares each
one with upstream byte for byte.

Written for DropOff from its files:

  Lifts.lua   the two ends of each lift, from the docks 1001 to 1014 of its
              Data/Transports.lua
  Walk.lua    the lookup of a hand-in's height follows its ns.SpawnHeight
              (Journey/JourneyInput.lua); the height of the Undercity's floor
              is the one given there

Where its data comes from, as its THIRD_PARTY.md says:

  Walking maps      The game client's terrain, baked with TrinityCore's tools
                    (GPL-2.0-or-later). Derived data, GPL-3.0-or-later;
                    Blizzard's EULA applies to the client files.
  Heights, lifts    CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0), and the WoW: Forever client
                    tables read through wago.tools (Blizzard Entertainment's
                    game data).
